    Règlement 131-2026

    In force

    New Ormstown bylaw makes dog and cat licences valid for life, sets conditions for backyard hens, and spells out what happens to dangerous dogs

    Bylaw 131-2026, in force since April 17, 2026, sets the rules for keeping dogs, cats and other pets in Ormstown: a licence valid for the animal's life, conditions for keeping up to 5 hens in the urban perimeter, and separate rules for dogs that are "at risk", "potentially dangerous" or "dangerous".

    Règlement 2-2026

    In force

    How Ormstown council meetings work under Bylaw 2-2026

    Règlement 2-2026, in force since April 17, 2026, sets the rules for Ormstown council meetings: two public question periods at every ordinary meeting, a 3-hour limit on a meeting, and a suspensive veto for the mayor that council can lift.

    Read the Observer explainer

    Règlement 80-2022

    In force

    Gifts, conflicts of interest, confidentiality: the rules Ormstown's municipal staff must follow

    Bylaw 80-2022, in force since May 4, 2022, is the code of ethics and conduct for the town's employees. A breach can lead to a sanction, from a verbal notice up to dismissal.

    Règlement 152-2023

    In force

    Repainting your house or adding a window? In parts of Ormstown, that needs council approval first

    Règlement 152-2023 requires council's approval of a site-planning and architectural-integration plan (PIIA) for certain work in the architectural core, in the Route 201 Sud and C-7 sector and in zone R-37 (rue Isabelle), and for any detached accessory dwelling unit anywhere in the municipality. Fines run from $500 to $4,000 for each day an offence lasts.

    Read the Observer explainer

    Règlement 145-2023

    In force

    Want to tear down a building in Ormstown? A committee of three council members decides, and anyone can object

    Bylaw 145-2023, in force since August 31, 2023, prohibits demolishing a building, in whole or in part, without the authorization of a demolition committee made up of three council members. Anyone may file a written objection within 10 days of the public notice, and the fine for demolishing a principal building without authorization starts at $10,000. Annex A, the heritage inventory the bylaw refers to, reads “(en attente de la MRC du Haut-Saint-Laurent).”

    Read the Observer explainer

    Règlement 147-2023

    In force, amended

    Ormstown's urban plan: 11 land-use designations that the zoning bylaw must respect

    Bylaw 147-2023, the plan d'urbanisme, divides the territory into 11 land-use designations, sets minimum housing densities and says development is to be concentrated in the urban perimeter. It sets no fines; the zoning bylaw must respect it. The copy the town has posted shows no adoption or in-force date and does not include Bylaw 147.1-2025, adopted April 13, 2026.

    Read the Observer explainer

    Règlement 155-2023

    In force

    Ormstown's fire bylaw: a free permit for open fires, town approval for fireworks, and rules for smoke alarms

    Bylaw 155-2023, in force since June 6, 2023, requires a free burning permit for open fires, the municipality's authorization to use fireworks, and a smoke alarm less than 10 years old on every storey of a building. Fines start at $300.

    Règlement 148-2023, ch. 12

    In force, amended

    Cutting down a tree in Ormstown? You need a permit for that

    Ormstown's rules on cutting and protecting trees are in chapter 12 of zoning bylaw 148-2023 and in permits bylaw 151-2023. Chapter 12 also covers shorelines, flood plains, drinking-water intakes, snow dumping and contaminated land. Felling a tree of 30 cm in diameter in contravention of the bylaw carries a fine of at least $500, plus an amount per tree or per hectare.

    Read the Observer explainer

    Règlement 86-2024

    In force

    The last digit of your address decides which day you can water your lawn

    Règlement 86-2024 sets the rules for using drinking water from Ormstown's distribution network: lawn-watering days by the last digit of the address, pool-filling dates by even or odd civic number, spring driveway washing between May 1 and June 15 only, and a February 1, 2027 deadline to replace air conditioning, refrigeration systems and compressors that use drinking water. Fines start at $250, and each day of a continuing offence counts as a separate offence.

    Read the Observer explainer

    Règlement 167.1-2026

    In force

    Public notice bylaw amended: new posting locations

    Municipality can no longer use its previously designated posting location. Public notices now appear on the municipal website and the exterior bulletin board at Town Hall (5, rue Gale). No standalone explainer yet.
