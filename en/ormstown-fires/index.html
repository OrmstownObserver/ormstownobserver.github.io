Ormstown’s suspicious fires: what is known so far

Developing Last updated Nov. 9, 2026

CBC counts four suspicious fires in Ormstown since July, the latest of which destroyed Pizzeria Ormstown on rue Lambton on November 8. The Sûreté du Québec is investigating and has not said whether the fires are connected.

This page is the running file. It lists the dates, links to every Observer story and video in order, and is updated as officials and police say more. Every fact is attributed to the source that reported it. If you have footage or information, give it to the Sûreté du Québec first.

Key dates

  1. July 20, 2026 Casa Venezia, rue Lambton, is destroyed by fire (The Gleaner).
  2. Sept. 19, 2026 A deliberately set fire damages the exterior of Express 57 (The Gleaner; CTV and CP24 date it Sept. 20).
  3. Nov. 4, 2026 A Molotov cocktail is thrown at the entrance of Pizzeria Ormstown; the damage is minor and one person is arrested (The Gleaner).
  4. Nov. 8, 2026 Pizzeria Ormstown is destroyed; the SQ treats it as a suspected arson (CBC).
  5. Nov. 9, 2026 The mayor meets Québec’s security minister; the SQ is to present an action plan to the municipality (CBC video).
