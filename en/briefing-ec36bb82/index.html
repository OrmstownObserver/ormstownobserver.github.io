Fête nationale + Canada Day: the outside organizer quadrupled the cost

Source: Annexe A payment lists, Jun 2 – Aug 4 2025 and Jun 1 – Aug 3 2026.

In 2025 the town booked five performers and vendors itself, with no outside organizer. In 2026 it paid Groupe Charlebois as well, and direct event costs still rose 44 %. On Dec 17 2025, the night the Charlebois mandate passed, resolution 25-12-367 moved 70 784,37 $ of unspent 2025 recreation salaries to other uses.

The Charlebois numbers don't add up. The June 1 2026 list labels the first payment, 29 893,50 $, « 1er vers 50% ». That implies a 59 787,00 $ contract with taxes (exactly 52 000 $ before tax), with 23 914,80 $ still to come. The Aug 3 payment, 5 978,70 $, is labelled « 20% » but equals exactly 10 %. Both payments were already issued when council approved the lists. The agreement is not public.
The September event council never saw. The Fête citoyenne (Sept 26, Parc Lindsay-Cullen) has no resolution in any minutes from Aug 3 to Oct 6, and no payment on the Sept 8 or Oct 6 lists. A Montréal company, Animation Concept, supplied the games, DJs and hosts. By contrast, the much smaller giant mandala for the Journées de la culture got its own logistics resolution (26-09-254). The company was likely paid through Groupe Charlebois, or its invoice will appear on the Nov 2 list.

The snow dump: awarded to one company, paid to another

On Nov 17 2025 council awarded the full emptying of the Wellington snow dump to E360 Solutions for 41 332,49 $, taxes and contingency included (25-11-315). E360 has received no payment. Services de Rebuts Soulanges Inc. was paid for « nettoyage dépôt à neige » instead: 23 224,96 $ (Nov 17 2025), then 21 519,11 $ and 12 660,55 $ (Jan 19 2026), 57 404,62 $ in total, 16 072,13 $ more than the award. To check first: whether E360 is a trade name of Services de Rebuts Soulanges.

Monthly approvals that state only part of the list

Each month council adopts "the list in Annexe A". In these four sittings, the amount written in the resolution (25-11-304, 26-06-150, 26-07-187, 26-10-268) is only one block of that list. The other eight sittings state the full grand total.

Before drawing conclusions. Some contracts may have been awarded before 2025, garbage collection especially. Check SEAO and the town's list of contracts over 25 000 $ (Code municipal, art. 961.3–961.4). The delegation by-law may let staff award some purchases without a resolution. Draft by-law 129-2026 is rewriting those rules now.

Questions council may wish to ask

  1. Why was Services de Rebuts Soulanges paid for snow-dump work awarded to E360 Solutions, and for 16 072 $ more than the award?
  2. Under which resolution or contract are the garbage collection, snow removal, road marking and software support paid? When do they expire?
  3. What are the rent, term and lessor of the library and garage leases, and the ceiling of the court-proceedings legal mandate (25-10-289)?
  4. Will monthly payment resolutions state the full Annexe A grand total from now on?
  5. Will by-law 129-2026 require a resolution with an amount for any contract or supplier over 25 000 $ a year?
