About the Observer

Privacy

Updated October 16, 2026

The Observer doesn’t track you. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies and no accounts. This page lists everything the site does touch, so you can check for yourself.

What we collect when you read

Nothing. We don’t run analytics, advertising networks or social-media pixels, and the site sets no cookies of its own. We can’t tell who reads which story.

What your own device keeps

A few small settings are saved in your browser so the site remembers them. They stay on your device and are never sent to us:

  • your language, English or French;
  • light or dark reading mode;
  • your language and your place in the Municipal Ledger.

Your browser also keeps copies of pages and photos you have opened, so they still load when you’re offline. Clearing this site’s data in your browser settings, or deleting the app from your home screen, removes all of it.

Outside services your browser contacts

Like most websites, some parts of the site load from other companies. When they do, your browser sends them your IP address and basic browser details, as any web request does. Their own privacy policies apply, and their servers may be outside Québec.

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts the site. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces.
  • Cloudflare’s cdnjs supplies the chart library used by the Municipal Ledger.
  • YouTube plays council-meeting video embedded in a few articles. YouTube may set its own cookies when the player loads.

Links to other sites, such as ormstown.ca or LégisQuébec, take you to pages we don’t control.

When you email us

If you write to ormstownobserver@gmail.com, we receive your address and your message. Our email is hosted by Google (Gmail). We use what you send to reply and to report. We never name the person behind a tip without their permission.

Story alerts

We plan to offer alerts when a new story is published. They will be off unless you turn them on. If you do, we will store:

  • the push address your browser creates for alerts. It is a long, random-looking web address from Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft that lets us reach that one device;
  • the topics you chose, such as Council Watch;
  • your language;
  • the date you signed up and the date of the last alert.

We don’t ask for your name, email, phone number or location. We use this information only to send the alerts you asked for, and we never sell or share it. It is kept on the Observer’s own server in Québec.

Each alert is encrypted before it leaves us, so the company that delivers it (Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft) can’t read it. That company does know an alert was delivered to your device.

To stop, turn alerts off in the app, or turn off notifications for the Observer in your phone’s settings. We delete a push address as soon as it stops working, and in any case when you turn alerts off.

Your rights

Under Québec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, you can ask what personal information we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted. Write to ormstownobserver@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

Who is responsible

The person responsible for protecting personal information at the Observer is the publisher, reachable at ormstownobserver@gmail.com.

If you aren’t satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec.

Changes

If this changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
